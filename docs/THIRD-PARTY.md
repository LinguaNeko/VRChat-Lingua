# Third-party components

[Project home](../README.md) · [User guide](USAGE.md)

- The headset overlay uses Valve OpenVR. See the bundled OpenVR notice in **Settings → Preferences**.
- Built-in speech uses Kokoro-82M for Mandarin, Supertonic 3 for the other built-in languages, sherpa-onnx, and ONNX Runtime. The corresponding license texts and notices are available under **Outgoing voice → Voice licenses**. The non-Mandarin built-in voices are subject to the bundled Supertonic terms.
- VB-CABLE is a separate driver distributed by [VB-Audio](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/); it is not part of the VRChat Lingua installer.
- VRChat Lingua is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by VRChat or Valve.
